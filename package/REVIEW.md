# ArenaGauntlet 1.0.2 review notes

Arena Career adds Armory, Barracks, Patronage and six optional Ascensions. Standard retains the existing ten rounds, horde population caps, gold rewards, prices and company damage. Career choices apply at run entry; the menu appears only on ArenaGauntlet survivor selection. MetaMarket rewards can stack.

Armory eventually supports five chosen items plus equipment, including eligible modded items, lunar gear and up to two reds, within 21 reusable points. Barracks provides a free tier-I company, a personal extra company slot and one commander recovery per run. Patronage provides one free white purchase per break, later white or green, outside paid round limits. Glory banks after each clear and survives a later wipe.

## Live checks required

- 1.0.2 multiplayer: a remote client that takes longer than 8 seconds to load into the arena (a slow PC or a large modpack) still starts with their own career ranks and loadout. A client whose career never arrives gets the default career 8 seconds after they finish loading, and the round still starts. Rounds against a modded champion with a very long name keep updating the client's HUD.
- 1.0.1 prices: a career saved by 1.0.0 shows the same unspent Glory and keeps its ranks and cosmetics. The next upgrade or cosmetic charges the new price. Giant-Slayer is awarded for the player's own killing blow on a champion but not for a company's, turret's or drone's. Unbroken is not awarded for rounds 1-4.
- 1.0.0 Treasury: buy each skin and decoration set (50 Glory each since 1.0.1). The Balance drops, buying selects or shows the item, and choices survive a restart. Check a remote client sees the host's choices, including after joining mid-run, and that their own choices do not apply when they are not hosting. Watch the load time entering an arena with all five decoration sets shown.
- 1.0.0 loadout tooltips: hovering a slot or any item/equipment choice (including unaffordable ones) shows the native tooltip above the career panel, with the full description, equipment cooldown and, with LookingGlass installed, its item stats. Completing one announces it in chat once, with the right player's name, and the Glory and COMPLETE mark persist after the run on host and remote client.
