# ArenaGauntlet

> **ArenaGauntlet has moved to GitHub.** 1.0.3 is the last update posted on Thunderstore. New versions will be at https://github.com/Kuriyukihime/ArenaGauntlet/releases. To get notified, click **Watch → Custom → Releases** on that page. To install a new version, import its ZIP in your mod manager (r2modman: Settings → Import local mod).

**Requires Survivors of the Void to host. Other expansions are optional.**

Survive ten rounds in a Greek stone colosseum, recruit allied companies, and build your survivor between battles. Each round brings a mixed horde followed by champions. Collect your clear reward, visit the south-gate merchants, and ready up for the next fight. The entire gauntlet takes place in the same arena.

## How to play

1. Install through Thunderstore and select **ArenaGauntlet** in the game-mode menu. Everyone in multiplayer needs the same version.
2. Choose your survivor and difficulty. Open **Arena Career** on this selection screen to spend Glory, prepare starting gear and view Ascension. This menu is only available while selecting ArenaGauntlet.
3. Each player begins with **three random white or green items**, replaced or expanded by their Armory loadout. The notification **Starting items have been granted!** confirms arrival rewards. The opening battle starts automatically after the party arrives.
4. Defeat the horde and its champions. After each clear, merchants open, dead teammates return, and the combat clock pauses. In multiplayer, loot is personal: each drop belongs to one player, and only they can see and pick it up.
5. Follow the arrow to the **brazier** and interact to ready up. Everyone must agree before the short countdown starts. Interact again to cancel your vote.

A party wipe ends the attempt. Glory already earned from completed rounds stays yours.

## Five merchant alcoves

- **Items:** six gold-priced choices, including eligible modded items. Each player can buy **three whites, two greens and one red per break**, shared across all normal-item displays, including duplicates.
- **Lunar items:** six choices costing **two lunar coins each** from your normal balance. Regular random lunar coin drops remain active.
- **Equipment:** six gold-priced choices to complement your build.
- **Drones:** recruit gunner, healing or missile drones. These offers are shared by the party.
- **Scrap trader:** exchange **one green scrap for the offered green item**, or **one red scrap for the offered red item**. A scrapper stands nearby.

Stock and purchase allowances refresh each break. Lunar, equipment and scrap offers allow one purchase per player per display. Pick up bought items and scrapper output normally; automatic enemy-loot collection leaves these for you. Finish scrapping before readying up.

Gold arrives in your ordinary wallet when you clear a round. Rewards grow with merchant prices and budget for items, equipment and drones. Enemies give no base gold; item effects can provide their own rewards.

## Build your arena career

Earn **Glory** for every completed round, with larger awards for rounds five and ten. A Standard clear pays **20 Glory**. Spend it on permanent upgrades; your selected starting loadout uses a reusable point budget, so choosing gear costs no Glory each attempt. Hover over any loadout choice to see its full description and stats (calculated per stack with LookingGlass installed).

- **Armory:** choose white and green starters, then unlock equipment, lunar items, red items and extra item slots. Fully upgraded, bring **five items plus equipment**, spending up to **21 points**, with at most **two reds**. Eligible modded items appear alongside native items. Blank or unavailable item slots roll white/green; a missing mod does not erase saved choices.
- **Barracks:** start with a free Cinder Warband or Chitin Phalanx, unlock **one additional company per player**, and eventually recover your first fallen commander once per run.
- **Patronage:** claim **one free white item each shopping break**, later choosing white or green. **This extra purchase never counts against your normal round item limits.**

**Feats** are twelve one-time challenges worth **140 Glory** in total: clear your first round, defeat the great champion, clear rounds without falling, slay champions, command companies, shop the Bazaar, carry a hoard of items and win, including on Ascension. Each pays the moment you achieve it.

The **Treasury** sells cosmetic looks for the colosseum, **50 Glory** each: three arena skins with matching floors (Gilded Basalt, Moonlit Sanctuary, Overgrown Ruins) and five decoration sets you can show or hide. Cosmetics never change gameplay. In multiplayer, everyone sees the host's choices.

Career progress is saved per local game profile. MetaMarket can be used alongside it; both systems can grant their rewards.

## Command your allies

Press **B** for the War Council. Spend **renown** from kills on commanders and their squads. Hired-unit kills credit their owner; champion kills reward every connected player. Renown is separate from shopping gold.

Ten companies provide firepower, healing, barriers and battlefield control. Living commanders replenish squads between rounds; fallen companies can be hired again. The council remains available during combat.

Tiers unlock on rounds **1/2/4/6/8**, costing **20/40/80/140/220 renown**. Base company limits are **three solo**, **two per player with 2-3 players**, and **one with four or more**. Barracks adds one to its owner's allowance. A lower host limit can apply. Party-size withdrawals refund hiring costs; manual discharges do not.

## Choose your challenge

Round **five** brings a Twisted Scavenger; round **ten** brings Mithrix. Ordinary rounds **6-9** have two champions.

Clear Standard to unlock **Ascension I**, then defeat each rank to unlock the next. The host chooses the party's challenge. Six ranks add stronger enemies, marked arena strikes and extra ordinary champions, rewarding **25-50 Glory** per clear. Progression stays effective in Standard; difficulty never automatically follows your career upgrades.

Horde counts retain their normal limits: round ten has **80/120/160/200 enemies with 1/2/3/4 players**, with at most 80 alive. Round ten ends in victory by default; enable endless continuation for another cycle. Host options are available through Risk of Options.
